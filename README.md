# Akan-name-generator
**Author:**Peace
A website for generating the day you were born and your Akan name from the Ghanian culture
generated when you fill out your birthday information on the form provided.

# Description
This website is an Akan name generator website it genrates the day you were born
in and your corresponding Akan name based on the gender that you are.
This website contains different sections which include:

-The first section which has the name of the website and  a short
description about the website
-The Form section this is where you put your day,month and year
of birth and also your gender and it also contains the submit button
to submit your information
-The Result section when you submit your information this is where the 
results appear which is the day you were born in and your Akan name.
