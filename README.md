# Akan-name-generator
**Author:**Peace
A website for generating the day you were born and your Akan name from the Ghanian culture
generated when you fill out your birthday information on the form provided.

# Description
This website is an Akan name generator website it genrates the day you were born<br>
in and your corresponding Akan name based on the gender that you are.<br>
This website contains different sections which include:<br>

-The first section which has the name of the website and  a short<br>
description about the website<br>
-The Form section this is where you put your day,month and year<br>
of birth and also your gender and it also contains the submit button<br>
to submit your information<br>
-The Result section when you submit your information this is where the <br>
results appear which is the day you were born in and your Akan name.<br>
